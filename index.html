What to expect when starting Linux from Scratch

Linux from scratch is a unique way to learn the Linux commands and what they do. It is also a great way to learn how Linux works.

This is a very long project. If you are new to Linux, do not expect this to take overnight. For me, it took 3 months to get halfway, and to this day I am still not finished. So take your time, be patient, and for some packages like GCC, feel free to walk away and come back in a few hours. For me in Chroot, GCC took 15 hours to complete its installation. So don't feel like you have to watch the process the whole time—take breaks!

What will break when doing Linux from Scratch

95% of breaks that happen in this project are due to mistyping a command. Double-check the command you are typing before hitting enter.
